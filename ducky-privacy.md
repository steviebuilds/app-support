# Ducky privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026. This policy describes Ducky version 2.0, provided by Wahlu Labs Pty Ltd.

## Data stored on your device

Ducky stores your coin balance, unlocked hats, best score, sound and display preferences, and a record of delivered purchase transaction identifiers on your device. These records support saved progress and prevent the same purchase from being credited twice. Ducky does not send these records to a server operated by us. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

## Purchases and Game Center

Optional purchases use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes payment and manages your purchase history. Ducky receives verified purchase information needed to deliver coin packs or recognize an existing ad-free purchase; we do not receive your payment-card details.

If you use Game Center, Ducky uses Apple's service for its leaderboard and submits your best score. Your Game Center account and leaderboard visibility are managed by Apple and your Game Center settings. These Apple services are subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Ads, analytics and tracking

Version 2.0 does not request or display advertisements. Its advertising SDKs are not started. New Remove Ads purchases are disabled while ads are off. Ducky does not use developer-operated analytics, tracking, accounts or a developer-operated backend in this version.

## Your choices

You can reset local progress by removing the app and its local data. This also removes locally stored coins and hats; consumable coin packs cannot be restored. Apple's purchase records, Game Center information and device backups are managed separately through Apple. Restore Purchases can restore a verified, active prior ad-free purchase with the Apple Account that bought it.

## Support and updates

If you voluntarily contact us, we use the information you provide to answer your request. The [public support page](https://github.com/steviebuilds/app-support) explains how to report a problem without disclosing private information. GitHub operates that support channel under its own privacy policy.

We will update this policy if a future Ducky release changes these practices, including if advertising is re-enabled.
